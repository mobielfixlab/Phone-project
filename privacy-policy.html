Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Mobile Fix Lab LLC (“Mobile Fix Lab,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our website, contact us, request a quote, submit a form, or interact with our advertising.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Device type or model
  • Information about the service you are requesting
  • Messages or other information you submit through contact or lead forms

We may also receive basic website and advertising information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and interactions with our advertisements through services such as Meta and Google.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to questions and quote requests
  • Contact you regarding your inquiry
  • Provide information about our products and services
  • Schedule or discuss services
  • Improve our website, advertising, and customer experience
  • Maintain business records
  • Prevent fraud or misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We collect only information reasonably necessary for our business purposes. The FTC recommends that businesses limit collection and retention of personal information to what they actually need.

Meta Lead Forms

If you submit information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, Meta may provide the information you entered to Mobile Fix Lab so that we can respond to your request.

Submission of a lead form does not automatically enroll you in recurring promotional text messages or marketing emails unless you separately agree to receive them.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, website, communications, advertising, or payment systems when reasonably necessary to provide those services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The FTC recommends that businesses understand what personal information they maintain, retain only what they need, protect it appropriately, and securely dispose of information that is no longer needed.

Cookies and Advertising

Our website and advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website activity, measure advertising performance, and improve our marketing.

Third-party services such as Google, Facebook, and Instagram may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You may contact us to:

  • Ask what information you have provided to us
  • Request that inaccurate information be corrected
  • Ask us to delete information when appropriate
  • Ask us to stop sending marketing communications

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe option included in those communications.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Federal COPPA requirements apply to websites and online services directed to children under 13 or that knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
